Journal Article · AI Engineering

Kumar, S.; Bist, S.H.
AI Engineering, 2026, 2(2), 13 · Published 12 Aug 2026 · DOI: 10.53941/aieng.2026.100013

An integrated AI security pipeline combining statistical drift detection (KS-Test, PSI, ADWIN), adversarial robustness evaluation (FGSM, PGD, DeepFool), and governance auditing (Giskard) into a single framework — showing that iterative and geometry-aware attacks expose far deeper CNN vulnerabilities than one-shot perturbations, and that drift monitoring can catch distributional shifts before deployment failures occur.
